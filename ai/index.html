AI use in The Ghostwriter Files

Some pictures and sample texts in this game were made with AI tools and reviewed by a person.

This game is about telling AI-made content from human-made content, so we are open about our own. The character portraits and some of the pictures you are asked to judge were generated with AI image tools. Some of the fake reviews, posts and messages in the cases were first drafted with an AI model, then edited by a person. We don't say which items are which, because that is the puzzle. All people, businesses and brands in the game are invented. No AI runs while you play.

The same text is in the game under Settings > Credits and licences. Questions: Loupewright@proton.me.
