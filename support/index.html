The Ghostwriter Files: Support

Need help with the game? Start here. If you can't find an answer, email us at Loupewright@proton.me. We read every message and aim to reply within 5 working days.

Quick answers

I lost my progress. Can I get it back?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no cloud save, so we can't see or restore it for you.

  • If you restart or update the app, nothing is lost.
  • If you deleted the app, the progress went with it. If you restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup that included the app, your progress may come back. That is controlled by Apple and your device settings, and we can't promise it will work.
  • If you moved to a new iPhone with Apple's transfer or backup tools, progress usually moves with it. Starting fresh on a new iPhone without them means starting again.
  • Settings > Reset saved progress erases your progress on this device on purpose.

How do hearts work? Mine aren't refilling.

  • You have up to five hearts. A wrong answer costs one.
  • When you have fewer than five, they come back one every 10 minutes. The home screen shows "Next heart in" with a countdown under your hearts.
  • Closing a case (solving its final level for the first time) fills all five.
  • Solving an optional or weekly file on your first try earns one heart back, once per file.
  • Replaying a level you have already solved costs no hearts, and neither do the optional files.
  • In Case 1, if you run out, take a short break and all five hearts come back.
  • The timer uses your iPhone's clock. If you changed the date or time, the count can look different. Setting your date and time to "Set Automatically" fixes most cases.

There is nothing to buy in the game and no ads. If you run out of hearts, wait for the timer, replay solved levels or read your notebook.

I think an answer in a case is wrong, or I found a mistake.

The game teaches "tells", which are clues, not proof. Every level is designed so that the answer can be found from what is on the screen and in your notebook. If you think a level has an error, tell us the case name, the level number, what you chose and what you think is right. Settings > Send feedback fills in the app version and the screen you were on for you.

Can the game tell me whether something I received is AI-written or fake?

No. It is a game, not a detector. Its tells are rules of thumb, and a real person can look like an AI. Please don't use it to judge or accuse a real person. See our Terms of Use.

Is the art AI-generated?

Some of it is. As the game says in Settings > Credits and licences: "Some pictures and sample texts in this game were made with AI tools and reviewed by a person." No AI runs while you play.

Which iPhones does it need?

An iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later. The game is made for iPhone and plays in portrait. There is no separate iPad version.

Something is broken or the app crashed.

Please update to the latest version, restart your iPhone, and try again. If it still fails, email us (or use Settings > Send feedback) with: your iPhone model, iOS version, app version (at the bottom of Settings), what you were doing, and a screenshot if possible.
