Privacy Policy: The Ghostwriter Files

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who we are

The Ghostwriter Files (the "app" or "the game") is made and published by Rachael Esther Stuart (Loupewright), an individual developer based in the Netherlands ("we", "us").

Questions about this policy or your privacy: Loupewright@proton.me.

2. The short version

  • We do not collect any data through the app. No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and nothing to buy.
  • Your progress stays on your iPhone. The game never sends it to us or to anyone else.
  • The game works offline. It makes no internet connections of its own.
  • If you email us, we receive what you send, and we use it only to answer you.

3. What the app stores on your iPhone

To remember where you are, the game saves the following in the app's own storage on your device:

  • which cases and levels you have solved, your results, and which hints you used;
  • your hearts and the time they last refilled;
  • which story scenes, tips and puzzles you have already seen;
  • tools you have unlocked in the story, and your settings.

This information never leaves your device. We have no server for the game, so we cannot see it, copy it or restore it.

How to delete it:

  • Settings > Reset saved progress in the game erases your progress on this device.
  • Deleting the app erases everything the game stored.
  • If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include the game's data, and restoring that backup may bring it back. Apple and your device settings control this, not us.

4. What we do not collect

The app does not collect or send: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, health data, browsing history, advertising identifier, or any other identifier. It does not ask for any device permission. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software, and no in-app purchases.

The game does not use AI while you play. All cases, pictures and texts are written in advance and included in the app.

5. Links that leave the app

Settings has links to this privacy policy and to our support page. Tapping one opens the page in your web browser. Settings > Send feedback opens your own email app with a new message to us (see section 6); nothing is sent unless you send it.

Our web pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. GitHub decides how long it keeps this information, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking on these pages, and they load nothing from other websites.

6. When you email us

If you write to Loupewright@proton.me, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach (for example a screenshot). If you use Settings > Send feedback, the new email is already filled in with the app version, your iOS version and the screen you were on (for example the case and level), so we can find the problem. You can read and change all of it before you send it.

We use this only to answer you and to fix problems you report. We keep support emails for 12 months after our last reply, then delete them, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Your email is handled by our email provider, Proton AG (Switzerland), on our behalf. Switzerland is recognised by the European Commission as giving personal data an adequate level of protection.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in answering your request and improving the game.

7. Apple

You download the game from Apple's App Store. Apple handles downloads, updates, ratings and reviews, and (if you join a test) TestFlight. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us crash reports and statistics about the app. Apple collects and controls this information under Apple's own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). What we receive from Apple does not identify you.

8. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not collect data from children either. If a child has emailed us and you want that email deleted, write to Loupewright@proton.me.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere) you can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it. In practice the only personal data we can hold is support emails you sent us. Write to Loupewright@proton.me and we will reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). You can also contact the authority where you live.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if a future version adds ads or purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released, change the "Last updated" date above, and describe the change in the App Store release notes.
